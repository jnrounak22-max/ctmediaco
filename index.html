About us ↘

About us

ChaosTherapy Media

How it started. We kept seeing talented brands with great products getting lost in too many tools, opinions and content with no point of view.

Why we built it. Modern brands need one team for strategy, creative, web and growth, not scattered pieces. So we became a creative growth studio that helps brands look better, communicate better and win customers.

Why the name. Every brand starts as a mess of ideas. That's the chaos. The therapy is us sitting with it, asking the hard questions and giving it shape. Our logo shows it: loose petals resolving into one mark.

What we believe
  • Strategy before execution
  • Creativity with a clear purpose
  • Consistency over random posting
  • Brand identity across every touchpoint
  • Design that supports business objectives
  • Continuous improvement instead of guesswork
